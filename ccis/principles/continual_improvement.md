# Continual improvement

Constitutional question: **Is the new state better, or at least defensibly safer?**

An accepted candidate must demonstrate improvement against the declared objective or a defensible reduction of risk. Applicable geometry, physics, numerical behavior, performance, regressions, and uncertainty are evaluated and preserved. Unknowns escalate; they do not silently pass.
