# Rollback and recovery

Constitutional question: **What is the only legal next action?**

Every gate decision names exactly one legal next action. Accepted candidates may be staged only through explicit operator acceptance. Rejected isolated candidates are discarded only after the original state is hash-verified. Interrupted work resumes from the append-only event chain; disposable projections never outrank it.
